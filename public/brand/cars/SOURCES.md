# Car brand logos

Source: https://github.com/vehiclespecs/brand-logos
Revision: 3f0929e70e0a9a2a502063edc4b3e5c0146cba74

Downloaded manufacturer marks converted to locally hosted WebP with transparent backgrounds, maximum 240 × 160 px. Used for manufacturer identification. Logos remain trademarks of their respective owners. Repository metadata and curation are MIT licensed; see LICENSE.txt.

Unmatched manufacturers use a text monogram, rather than an unrelated logo. No customer vehicle data or database records are changed.
