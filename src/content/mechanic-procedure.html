Booking Procedure

  1. Smart Servicing Centre will contact you via email regarding any new jobs requests available in your area with details on the job, location and vehicle.
  2. You can accept or decline the booking. If you decline the booking you should give an alternative date and time whenever possible, and this may be forwarded to the customer.
  3. If you accept the job, all the job details will be pushed into your app.
  4. You must then show up at the booking location within the designated arrival Time Slot. Not arriving within the arrival Time slot is not acceptable unless circumstances arise that are beyond your control. If you cannot arrive in the arrival window you must call the customer as soon as you become aware to inform them of this and arrange either to show up later or another suitable time.
  5. Once you have arrived on the job you should undertake all work as specified in the original booking which will be found in your mobile application.
  6. If further work on the vehicle is needed, then this should be communicated to the customer. Please tell the customer what work is required on the vehicle and what is the cost to undertake the work.
  7. Any additional work which is required on the vehicle must be added in the app. (You can add extra labour and any additional part required into the Garage app with the approval of the customer)
  8. Once the work is complete press the (mark order Complete) button on the app and this will automatically email an invoice to the customer on your behalf and charge the full amount to their card.
  9. Your bank account will be credited for the earnings you have made within 5 working days.
  10. Smart Servicing Centre commission will be automatically deposited into our bank account.
  11. The customer will receive an automated review email which will give the customer the opportunity to review you and the Smart Servicing Centre experience. Your reviews are visible on your profile and will be displayed to potential customers in future.

Additional Work Procedure

  1. If a diagnostic, repair or service requires additional labour or parts then this should be communicated to the customer.
  2. The quote for additional labour, additional part or Both should be added in the Smart Servicing centre App with the approval of the customer. Please note that Smart Servicing centre will take 15% of the value of the parts.
  3. Once the work is done, Generate the invoice through the Smart Servicing centre App.

After Work Procedure

  1. Once all work is complete and you have invoiced the customer through the Smart Servicing Centre app the full amount will be charged against the customer's stored credit or debit card details.
  2. It will take approximately 5 working days for funds to clear and appear in your bank account. You can keep up to date with any transactions in your dashboard or download stripe app from app store to track the transection.
  3. The customer will receive an automated email asking to give us a feedback.
  4. If the mechanic / Garage receives low ratings on any Job Smart Servicing Centre will contact the mechanic / Garage to find out Reason, and any appropriate action will be taken from there.
  5. All customer reviews will be publicly displayed in future on your profile. This will offer a great opportunity to show what a great Garage/ mechanic you are and will help build trust with future customers.

Parts Ordering Procedure

  1. The Garage / mechanic may use their preferred parts suppliers and must collect (or have parts delivered) prior to the job.
  2. All parts should be taken to the site of the job and replaced.
  3. The customer, if present, should always have the option to see the parts that have been replaced and you, the mechanic / Garage, should ask whether they would like to see the replaced parts.

Diagnostic Procedure

Diagnostic procedure can either be an electronic plug in diagnostic or a Physical diagnostic which inspects various parts and areas of the vehicle.

  1. Any diagnostic jobs you accept should be attended in the same manner as outlined in the Booking Procedure.
  2. We give 1 hours for all our diagnostic inspections. In this time, you should diagnose the fault with the vehicle. If this is a minor fault which can be repaired without any additional parts and within the 1 hours diagnostic time then the fault should be repaired at no extra cost to the customer.
  3. If additional time will be required to diagnose the fault then this should be communicated to the customer as soon as you become aware and they can then decide whether they want you to continue diagnosing the fault.
  4. If the customer wants the diagnosis to continue then this should be charged for through the Smart Servicing Centre app. However, if the customer wishes for no further work to be undertaken then the customer should be given a full report on all the diagnostic work that have been undertaken to date and the initial diagnostic should be charged for through the app.
  5. Once a fault has been diagnosed the fault should be communicated to the customer.
  6. Full quote to repair the fault should be given to the customer and if the customer authorises the work, the quote for additional labour, additional part or Both should be added in the Smart Servicing centre App. Please note that Smart Servicing centre will take 15% of the value of the parts.
  7. Once the work is finished invoice the customer in a single invoice through the Smart Servicing Centre app.

Servicing Procedure

  1. Servicing jobs should be carried out according to the appropriate servicing schedule, schedules for full, interim and Majour services can be found in the 'service schedules' section.
  2. In the service schedules we expect fluids to be topped up, this is subject to fair-usage. Where larger quantities of fluid are required and additional costs arise, you may decide to charge an extra fee but please ensure this is cleared with the customer before you charge.
  3. After having carried out a service please provide a breakdown of the checks carried out and any relevant findings and recommendations on the booking receipt when you charge the job in the app.
  4. If the customer has noted in the booking that the servicing book needs to be stamped, please ensure you have your stamp with you on the job to stamp the book after the service has been carried out.

Payment and Refund Procedure

  1. Once you mark the order complete and charge the customer through Smart Servicing Centre App it takes approximately 5 working days for funds to clear and appear in your bank account. You can keep up to date with any transactions in your smart servicing centre dashboard or keep a track of all the payments through stripe app (available on app store).

Please Note:  Our promise to customers is that we do not take cash payments, in this way all payments remain transparent and above board, preventing potential confusion at later stages.

  1. If any later stage, the customer gets in touch with a complaint regarding the work carried out and demands a refund or partial refund. In this instance, please initially communicate with the customer directly to reach a satisfactory outcome for both parties.
  2. If a problem cannot be resolved between you, the mechanic/ garage, and the customer we will launch an investigation and attempt mediate to find a resolution to the dispute.
  3. Please ensure you communicate with us throughout the process to ensure a positive outcome for all parties involved.
  4. If, upon completion of the investigation, a refund or partial refund appears in order, we will action the refund on your behalf.
